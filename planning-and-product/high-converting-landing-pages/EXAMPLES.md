# Worked examples

Calibration artifacts, not templates to copy verbatim. Bracketed notes mark
where real assets must exist before the element ships.

## 1. SaaS free trial (product-aware traffic, short-medium form)

```
[NAV: logo only, no links]

[HERO]
Eyebrow: FOR REMOTE ENGINEERING TEAMS
Headline: Ship faster with async standups.
Subheadline: Replace the daily sync with 30-second video check-ins.
  AI surfaces blockers so nobody reads transcripts.
CTA: [Start my free trial]   micro: No credit card. Set up in 3 minutes.
Trust: [logo bar — ONLY actual customers; omit entirely if none]
Visual: product screenshot of the standup summary view

[PROBLEM]
Your team spends 45 minutes every morning in a standup that could be a
30-second update. Engineers lose their flow state. Half the team is in
another timezone and watches the recording at 2x anyway.

[SOLUTION + MECHANISM]
StatusFlow replaces the meeting, not the alignment. Each person records a
30-second update when it suits them; the model extracts blockers, progress,
and risks into one morning digest. The mechanism: summaries are diffed
against yesterday's, so stalled work surfaces automatically.

[HOW IT WORKS]
1. Record — a 30-second update, on your own schedule
2. Digest — blockers and progress land in one Slack summary
3. Act — stalled threads get flagged before they slip a sprint

[PROOF — real testimonials only, specific result + name + title + company]
"We got back 5 hours per engineer per week." — [name, CTO, company]

[FEATURES AS BENEFITS: 4 items]
[FAQ: setup time, security, pricing, adoption, what happens after trial]

[FINAL CTA]
Give your team their mornings back.
[Start my free trial]   No credit card. Works with Slack, Jira, Linear.
```

## 2. D2C product (problem-aware traffic, long form)

```
[HERO]
Headline: Wake up with clear skin. Finally.
Subheadline: A dermatologist-formulated system, personalized to your skin —
  visible results in 4 weeks or your money back.
CTA: [Take the 60-second skin quiz]
Trust: [review count + rating — real figures only]

[PROBLEM]
You've tried cleansers, prescriptions, and internet routines. Skin clears
for a week, then breaks out again. You're tired of paying for shelf-fuls
of half-used products.

[SOLUTION + MECHANISM]
Clarity is a 3-step system matched to your skin type and history. Proven
actives (2% salicylic acid, niacinamide, retinol) at clinical
concentrations — the difference is the personalization, not a secret
ingredient.

[HOW IT WORKS]
1. Quiz — 60 seconds about your skin
2. Kit — your 3-step system, formulated and shipped
3. Results — visible improvement in 28 days, guaranteed

[PROOF]
Before/after photos [real, consented], video testimonials [real],
practitioner endorsements [named, real].

[OBJECTIONS]
Sensitive skin? · Already on prescription? · What if it doesn't work?
(60-day money-back) · Subscription trap? (cancel anytime, one click)

[FINAL CTA]
[Take the skin quiz]
Free shipping. Cancel anytime. 60-day money-back guarantee.
```

## 3. Pre-traction validation page (no proof exists — the case the
no-proof playbook covers)

Context: concierge pilot for a niche B2B workflow, zero customers, outbound
traffic. No testimonials, no logos, no metrics — and none invented.

```
[HERO]
Eyebrow: FOR SELL-SIDE ADVISORY FIRMS
Headline: Know which inbound mandates deserve a partner conversation.
Subheadline: A structured first-pass read of every seller submission —
  thesis fit, financial completeness, process readiness — before senior
  time is spent.
CTA: [Book a working session]   secondary: See how it works ↓
Visual: PRODUCT EVIDENCE — a concrete mocked UI of one realistic submission
  being screened (believable scenario data, clearly a product view; never
  fake customer names presented as real accounts)

[PROBLEM]
Good mandates get missed while incomplete ones consume senior time. When
first review runs on inbox order and individual judgment, the same
submission reads differently depending on who opens it.

[MECHANISM — carries the credibility that proof would normally carry]
The four questions every first pass answers, shown explicitly, with the
follow-up requests the system generates when information is missing.

[HOW IT WORKS: 3 steps, honest about the human-in-the-loop]

[HONEST SCOPE — replaces the social-proof section]
A fit: founder-led firms, 2–20 people, public seller intake.
Not a fit: bulge-bracket banks, buy-side-only advisors, debt brokers.

[PILOT FRAMING]
"This is a concierge pilot. We run the first pass together, by hand where
needed, and only build what proves useful. You keep everything we produce,
whatever you decide afterward."

[FINAL CTA — a working session, not a purchase]
Bring three recent submissions. We'll show the first-pass read we'd have
produced for each.
[Request a working session]
No deck, no demo environment. We reply within one business day.
```

Why this converts without proof: the specific mechanism signals competence,
the "not a fit" list signals honesty, the pilot framing matches reality,
and the CTA asks for a working session (proportional commitment), not a
contract.
